Expert trajectory

Leaderboard

Every policy faces the same enumerated tasks in the same order. oracle-replay is not a policy — it replays the expert through the action codec, so its score is the ceiling any policy could reach, and the gap between it and 1.000 measures what the codec cannot express.

Success against task difficulty

Compounding error, measured directly: the policy is handed an expert prefix and must supply the last k actions itself. Behavioural cloning holds up when one action is left and collapses by eight.

Paired comparisons

Bootstrap intervals on the per-task difference, recomputed from the traces in this bundle. Pairing matters at this sample size: most of the variance is between tasks, and pairing removes it. An interval that straddles zero means the benchmark cannot separate the two policies — which is the honest answer more often than not here.

Behavioural cloning

Held out by design, not by step. Splitting on steps would put an action from the same trajectory on both sides and inflate the number.

PPO

Clipped surrogate with a KL anchor back to the cloned policy. Reward is dense over milestones; success is the full-build rate on the training pool, which is why it sits well above the held-out benchmark score.

What the policy is actually using

Each row corrupts one input and re-runs the identical suite. shuffled-req pairs a design with another design's requirement; blank-req removes the text entirely; no-mask drops the action-legality mask.

Read these together. The requirement ablations confirm the policy reads its requirement rather than memorising a family prior. The mask ablation is less flattering: much of the low invalid-action rate belongs to the mask, not the policy.
